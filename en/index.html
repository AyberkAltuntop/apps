Asphalt Eye

Car Dashcam

Turns your iPhone into a dash camera. After an accident, a damage dispute or a traffic fine, you have a recording you can use as evidence.

Coming soon to the App Store · iPhone, iOS 26 or later

Your Recordings Stay on Your Phone

Your location, video and audio recordings are stored on your phone. They're never sent to us. No account, no server, no ads.

Recording

Put the phone in a mount and tap Start Recording. The app does the rest.

Loop recording: when storage fills up, the oldest videos are deleted. Locked clips are never deleted.

Short Loop: when on, only the last 2 minutes of video are kept. Many European countries require this for dashcams.

Date, time, speed and location stamped on every frame.

Events

While the phone is in a mount, hard braking, rapid acceleration, sharp turns and impacts are detected. The video of the moment is locked.

Evidence package: clip, PDF report, measurement data and SHA-256 fingerprints of the files, in one package.

Rental Car

A guided photo walkaround at pickup and return.

Pickup and return photos side by side, so new damage stands out.

Trips

Every trip on a map, with speed and G-force charts.

If you forget to open the app, Auto Trips still records your route without video.

Optional Driving Score.

Premium

Recording during navigation, the cabin camera, 4K recording, rental report sharing and more are part of Premium. Premium is available with Premium Yearly, Premium Lifetime or a 30-day Travel Pass.

Don't touch the phone while driving. The app isn't an emergency service. It doesn't call anyone automatically and may not detect every collision.
