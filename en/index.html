Turn Your iPhone Into a Dashcam

Turns your iPhone into a dash camera. After an accident, a damage dispute or a traffic fine, you have a recording you can use as evidence.

Coming soon to the App Store · iPhone, iOS 26 or later

Recording

Tap Start Recording

Put the phone in a mount and tap Start Recording. The app does the rest.

Loop Recording

When storage fills up, the oldest videos are deleted. Locked clips are never deleted.

Short Loop

When on, only the last 2 minutes of video are kept. Many European countries require this for dashcams.

Stamped on Every Frame

Date, time, speed and location stamped on every frame.

Events

The Moment Gets Locked

While the phone is in a mount, hard braking, rapid acceleration, sharp turns and impacts are detected. The video of the moment is locked. Loop recording won't delete it.

Privacy

Your Recordings Stay Yours

Your location, video and audio recordings are stored on your phone. They're never sent to us.
