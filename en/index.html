Asphalt Eye on a Trip

From Setting Out to Getting Home

  1. STOP 01

    Background Recording

    Keep Recording in the Background

    Switch to navigation. The camera keeps recording in a small window.

  2. STOP 02

    Landscape Recording

    However You Mount It

    Landscape or portrait. The screen rotates even while recording.

  3. STOP 03

    Events

    The Moment Stays Locked

    While the phone is in a mount, hard braking, rapid acceleration, sharp turns and impacts are detected. On an impact or hard braking, the video of that moment is locked automatically. To keep any other moment, tap Lock or ask Siri.

    After an impact, the checklist, photos and witness details are on one screen.

  4. STOP 04

    Evidence Package

    Report, Clip and Route in One Package

    Clip, PDF report, measurement data and SHA-256 fingerprints of the files, in one package. The package is prepared on your phone. It leaves your phone only if you share it.

  5. STOP 05

    Trips

    Every Trip on the Map

    Video, route, speed chart and events on one screen. All your trips on one map. Roads you use often look brighter.

    If you forget to open the app, Auto Trips still records your route without video.

  6. STOP 06

    Rental Mode

    Rental Car? Photo Walkaround First

    Photos from the same angles at pickup and return. Pickup and return photos side by side, so new damage stands out. You can share the rental report.

  7. ARRIVAL

    Driving Score

    What's Your Driving Score?

    A score from 0 to 100 based on braking, acceleration and cornering. Your score for the last 30 days sets your tier, from Bronze to Platinum. You can turn it off.

Privacy

Your Recordings Stay Yours

Your location, video and audio recordings are stored on your phone. They're never sent to us. Only you can share a recording.

  • No account
  • No server
  • No ads

If you turn on Recording Vault, locked clips and trips are also backed up to your own iCloud account. Privacy Policy

Don't touch the phone while driving. The app isn't an emergency service. It doesn't call anyone automatically and may not detect every collision.
