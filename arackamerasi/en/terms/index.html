What the App Does

The app turns your iPhone into a dashcam. It records the road, keeps track of your route and speed, and marks events such as hard braking and impacts. It helps you record the condition of a rental car with photos at pickup and return, and prepare a report and package after an incident.

Recordings contain information that can be used as evidence in an accident or dispute. But we don't decide how an authority, insurance company or court will assess a recording. The app doesn't guarantee any outcome.

Recording May Not Always Be Possible

The app can't guarantee that a moment will be recorded. For example, recording may fail or be incomplete when:

  • Camera, location or another permission hasn't been granted.
  • The phone has overheated, its battery is low or its storage is full.
  • The vehicle hasn't moved for a long time. If the recording screen is open, we ask first whether to keep recording.
  • You've left the app and Background Recording (Premium) isn't on. In that case, video stops and route recording continues.
  • iPhone has stopped the camera. For example, when the small window is hidden while the app is in the background, when the phone is locked, or when another app is using the camera.
  • The app has been closed or the phone has restarted.
  • With loop recording on, the oldest unlocked recordings are deleted when the storage limit is reached. With Short Loop on, unlocked footage is kept for 2 minutes at most.

To keep an important moment, lock the clip. Clips you lock aren't deleted by loop recording. With Short Loop on, clips locked automatically on hard braking, and clips of an impact you mark as "Not an Accident," are deleted after 3 days unless you keep them.

Driving Safety

  • Don't hold your phone, touch the screen or look at it while driving. Start recording before you set off. Check the results when you've stopped.
  • To keep a moment while driving, say "Lock clip with Asphalt Eye" to Siri. On an impact, the clip is locked automatically.
  • Watch recorded videos while the vehicle is stopped.
  • Mount the phone where it won't block your view or interfere with driving. The phone shouldn't keep you from seeing the road, traffic or signs. Follow local rules on where a device may be mounted. In some places, a phone may be mounted only in a lower corner of the windshield or on the dashboard.
  • Always follow traffic laws. The app's alerts and information don't replace careful driving.

Emergencies

The app isn't an emergency service. It doesn't call anyone on its own. It doesn't report an impact to other people or to any authority. It may not detect every collision. Impacts are detected only while a trip is being recorded and the phone is steady in a mount.

If anyone is hurt or you need help, call the local emergency number yourself. In some countries, the Incident Screen shows an emergency number. The country is found from the incident location. If the location is wrong or you're near a border, the number shown may not be the one for the country you're in. When you tap the call button, iPhone asks before calling.

The list on the Incident Screen is a general reminder. It isn't legal advice. Your legal duties at the scene of an accident, such as stopping, helping anyone who's hurt and reporting the accident to the authorities, don't depend on the app.

Lawful Use of Recordings

  • Other people, vehicles and license plates may appear in recordings. If audio recording is on, conversations are recorded. If the cabin camera is on, images of the people in the vehicle are recorded. You're responsible for using and sharing recordings lawfully.
  • Tell the people in the vehicle and get their permission before turning on audio recording or the cabin camera. Tell anyone new who gets in too.
  • Recording audio without permission may be a crime in Türkiye and in many other countries. Conversations recorded without permission may not be accepted as evidence. Several US states (for example California, Florida, Illinois, Maryland, Massachusetts, Pennsylvania and Washington) and several Australian states require the permission of everyone taking part in a conversation. Some Australian states also prohibit filming the people in a vehicle without their permission.
  • In some places, a vehicle with a camera must display a visible notice that it records or that conversations may be recorded.
  • Don't record other people's conversations when you're not in the vehicle. Turn off audio recording or stop recording when you get out.
  • You can give recordings to the police, a prosecutor, a court, your insurer or your lawyer.
  • Before publishing recordings online or on social media, make faces and license plates unrecognizable. Don't publish conversations, footage from inside the vehicle, injured people or footage showing people's private lives without their permission. In Türkiye, sharing footage on social media that encourages or praises breaking traffic rules is also prohibited.
  • Record and share witness details and document photos only with the person's permission.
  • If you use the app in a taxi, a shuttle, for carrying passengers, in a company vehicle or for other work, data protection laws give you more duties. Tell passengers and employees before recording.
  • In some countries, using a dashcam or sharing recordings is restricted or prohibited. Check the local rules before using the app in another country. You're responsible for following the rules of the country where you record.
  • In many European countries, for example Germany, Austria, Switzerland and Luxembourg, the road may be recorded only in short loops, and footage may be kept only after an incident. In these countries, turn on Short Loop in Settings. Even with Short Loop on, recordings may not be accepted as evidence in some countries.
  • Don't use the app to secretly monitor or harass others or for any unlawful purpose.

Measurements Are Approximate

  • Speed is measured from your phone's location (GPS). It may differ from your vehicle's speedometer and from radar readings. When the signal is weak, it may not be measured or may be shown as approximate.
  • Events (hard braking, rapid acceleration, hard cornering, impact), Driving Score, elevation, and the start and end of auto trips are calculated from your phone's sensors. They may be wrong or incomplete.
  • The date and time in videos and reports come from your phone's clock. The app tries to verify the time and states the result in reports. It doesn't guarantee that the time is correct.
  • Driving Score is calculated only from hard braking, rapid acceleration and hard cornering. The score doesn't show whether you follow speed limits or other traffic laws. It isn't an insurance, licensing or legal assessment.

Storing Recordings

  • Your recordings are stored on your phone. Videos, trips, photos and packages aren't included in your iPhone backup.
  • Recording Vault (Premium) isn't offered in Türkiye. Outside Türkiye, if you turn on Recording Vault, locked clips, trips, events, rentals and photos are also backed up to your own iCloud account. Unlocked loop recordings and evidence packages aren't included in the vault.
  • A recording is uploaded to the vault only when there's an internet connection and enough iCloud storage. Recordings that haven't been uploaded yet can be lost with the phone.
  • If Premium ends, recordings in the vault stay in your iCloud account, and new recordings aren't uploaded. You don't need Premium to restore from the vault.
  • If you delete the app, or your phone is lost or damaged, the recordings on your phone are lost too. Recordings that aren't in Recording Vault can't be recovered.
  • It's your responsibility to save recordings you want to keep to Photos or another safe place.

Rental Mode and Reports

Rental Mode helps you record the condition of a car with photos when you pick it up and return it. Reports can be used in a dispute with the rental company. But they don't guarantee an outcome. They don't replace your rental agreement or the company's handover form.

  • A report shows only the spots you photographed. Damage that isn't visible in the photos or that's found later may not be in the report.
  • You enter the odometer, fuel and damage notes yourself. You're responsible for entering them correctly.
  • Have any damage you see written on the company's handover form too. If you email the report to the company at pickup, it's easy to show that it existed on that date.
